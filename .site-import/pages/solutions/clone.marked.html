What Novera is not

A document that overstates itself is worth less — especially to the person you hand it to.

Testing evidence, not certification

This report records Novera's testing of the declared agent, scenarios and configuration at the time shown. It is not a legal opinion, a conformity assessment, a certification or an assurance that any law is met, and Novera is not a notified body.

  • Not a certification. Novera is evidence of testing.

  • Not legal advice. It does not say which obligations apply to your organisation.

  • One dated run. One suite version against one policy version.

  • Only the suite. Not your agent's behaviour on every conversation.

Every report carries the same notice and its own scope and limitations. Read the limits in full.
